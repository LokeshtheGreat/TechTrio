Clinical Medical Terms Reference

RestingBP (Resting Blood Pressure)

Resting systemic systolic blood pressure measured in millimetres of mercury (mm Hg) while relaxed. High blood pressure (>130 mm Hg) stresses arterial walls and heart muscle.

Serum Cholesterol

Total amount of cholesterol found in blood, measured in mg/dL. Elevated cholesterol (>200 mg/dL) contributes to atheroma plaque buildup inside coronary arteries.

ChestPainType (Angina Types)

TA: Typical Angina (tightness/pressure behind breastbone upon exertion).
ATA: Atypical Angina (unusual presentation).
NAP: Non-Anginal Pain (musculoskeletal or gastrointestinal).
ASY: Asymptomatic (no notable chest pain).

Fasting Blood Sugar (FastingBS)

Blood glucose after an overnight fast. Values above 120 mg/dL indicate metabolic dysfunction or diabetes, a significant risk factor for cardiovascular disease.

RestingECG (Electrocardiogram)

Graphic recording of electrical heart activity while resting. Detects past myocardial infarctions, ST-T segment abnormalities, or left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH).

Oldpeak & ST Slope

Oldpeak: ST segment depression measured relative to rest during maximal exercise stress test.
ST Slope: Orientation of the ST segment at peak exercise (Upsloping, Flat, or Downsloping). Flat or downsloping patterns frequently correlate with coronary ischemia.
